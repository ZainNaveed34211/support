What support covers

Support is for websites and services Kluzar has already built for you.

  • Help when something on your website or service is not working as expected
  • Minimum changes on request, such as text changes and photo replacement, within your plan
  • Questions about your service, your plan, and what is included in it

The exact level of after-launch management depends on your plan, and plan details are shown on the payments page.

Somewhere else?

Not every request is a support request. Use the right place, and you will get an answer faster.

  • A new website or new enquiry: use the contact page
  • Plans, subscriptions, and payments: visit payments.kluzar.com
  • Account sign-in: sign in through accounts.kluzar.com
  • Questions before you buy: see the FAQ page
